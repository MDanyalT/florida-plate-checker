Florida Plate Finder

A private research tool that runs on your computer. It checks plates by opening the official FLHSMV plate inquiry in a visible browser, a few plates at a time.

Good to know

Results show what the official site reported when you checked. Availability can change at any time, and this site can't reserve or order plates. To order, use Florida's official channels, such as your county tax collector's office.

Responsible checking

Visible browser · one small batch at a time · pauses between batches · you complete any human verification yourself · no hidden or scheduled checking.
